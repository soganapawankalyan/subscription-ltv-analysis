Recommendations

  1. Shift budget from paid social to referral and partnership. Referral returns roughly nine times more lifetime profit per dollar. Test with a capped change.
  2. Rework the promotion. A 50% discount that only delays cancellation costs revenue. Test a smaller or stepped discount.
  3. Pair price rises with a retention offer. The rise was net positive, but churn and downgrades spiked for three months.
  4. Invest in the first 90 days. Churn is highest in months 2 and 3, so onboarding is the cheapest place to improve retention.
  5. Test an early-upgrade nudge. Customers who upgrade early retain better (77.8% vs 65.3%, n = 288), but engaged customers may simply be the ones who upgrade.

Method. SQL (SQLite) builds MRR, churn, cohorts, CAC and diagnostics. Python fits Kaplan-Meier survival curves (customers still active in Dec 2025 are censored) and projects 60-month gross-profit LTV: observed curve to month 24, then constant churn at each channel's months 13 to 24 average. Gross margin of 70% is an assumption. Bootstrap intervals capture sampling noise only, not the tail assumption.

Limits. Mix-adjusted churn controls for channel only. Plan and channel overlap, so plan retention is not a pure plan effect. The price-rise estimate assumes excess churn was caused by the rise.
